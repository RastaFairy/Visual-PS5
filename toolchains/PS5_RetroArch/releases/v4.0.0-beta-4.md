- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

<a id="smb"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/cloud.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> SMB. Your games, from your PC.

Keep your backups on your PC and play them on the PS5 without copying them over. RetroArch's file browser, Load Content included, has a new **SMB** entry after INTERNAL and EXTERNAL.

1. Share a folder on your PC (Windows file sharing, or Samba on Linux and macOS).
2. Open **SMB** in the WebUI and enter your PC's name or address under **Server**, with your user name and password (leave the user name empty to sign in as a guest).
3. Choose **Test connection**, then the **Share** and the **Folder** that holds your games, and **Save**.
4. In RetroArch, open **Load Content → SMB** and start a game.

- **Most cores read the game straight from the share.** A core that opens files by itself gets a copy on the console first, removed when the game closes.
- **Your password stays on your PS5.** The WebUI never shows it back to you, and it is sent only to your PC.
- **Read-only.** Nothing on your share is changed.

SMB support is inspired by [itsPLK's PS5 Package Manager](https://github.com/itsPLK/ps5-pkg-manager) and uses the same libsmb2 revision.

<a id="frame-generation"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/video.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Frame Generation without the doubled picture

- **The frames made in between now match the real ones.** The GameCube core reported a smaller picture than it hands over, so every generated frame was a zoomed part of the real one. On screen the two alternated, which looked like a doubled image.
- **A game below full speed shows its real frames again.** When a core fell behind, Frame Generation could show only frames made in between, never the game's own.
- **Checked on every core.** Each core ran with Frame Generation on while every picture sent to the display was recorded and compared, generated with real. The results are below.

<a id="library"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/library.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Your library

- **A rename reaches every list.** A name you edit in the WebUI's Games page now shows in RetroArch's playlists (XMB and RGUI) and in EmulationStation, every time you change it.
- **Delete game.** The Files tab of a game in the WebUI deletes it from your PS5: the file, the track files its disc names, and its place in RetroArch and EmulationStation. Your saves and savestates are kept. When two copies share a name, the box art and details stay with the copy you keep.
- **RetroAchievements shows you as signed in.** After you sign in, RetroArch keeps a sign-in token instead of your password; the WebUI now shows it as saved instead of "Not set".

<a id="fixes"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/checkmark-circle.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Fixes

- **GameCube texture packs work.** Dolphin's custom texture cache had no memory on the PS5; it now has 512 MiB. Turn on **Load Custom Textures** in the Dolphin core options.
- **PSP: changing Internal Resolution in the Quick Menu no longer closes RetroArch.**
- **The WebUI's Update button works.** Every release now carries the checksum file the updater checks; v3.0.0-beta-3 has it too.

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from v3.0.0-beta-3

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content and any custom media.
2. **Use the WebUI's Update button**, or merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the package into your installation, keeping your own files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
