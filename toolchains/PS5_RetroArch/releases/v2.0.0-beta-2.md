It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

<a id="ps5sx2"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/systems/ps2.webp" width="26" height="26" alt=""> PlayStation 2, made for the PS5

**PS5SX2** replaces LRPS2. It is [Swordpdf's PS5SX2](https://github.com/Swordpdf/PS5SX2), the PCSX2 edition built around the PS5's hardware, brought into RetroArch as a core: its recompilers, its fixes, and its Vulkan renderer drawing on the same GPU device RetroArch uses.

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Internal Resolution** | Native, 2×, 3×, **4× (≈1440p, the default)**, 5× (≈1800p), **6× (≈2160p, 4K)**, 8× (≈2880p) |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 4:3 or 16:9 |
| **Renderer** | Hardware (Vulkan) or Software |
| **Fast Boot** | Start the game directly, or watch the BIOS intro first |

- **Save states** from the Quick Menu, like every other core.
- **RetroAchievements** for PS2 games, with the console's main memory and scratchpad exposed the way RetroAchievements reads them.
- **Rumble** on your DualSense when the game asks for it.
- **Your BIOS stays where it is.** Keep your own dumps in `system/pcsx2/bios`; PS5SX2 picks the newest USA one there, or the newest of any region.

**Use only a BIOS dumped from a PlayStation 2 you own.** Compatibility and performance vary by game.

<a id="frame-generation"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/video.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Frame Generation. Motion, smoothed.

A trained network (PS5_FrameGen) makes the frames between a game's own, so a 30 or 60 fps game reaches your screen at a higher frame rate. It is on by default, for every core.

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Frame Generation** | On or off. |
| **Target Framerate** | **120 fps:** a 60 fps game gets one made frame between each pair, a 30 fps game three. **60 fps:** a 30 fps game gets one; a 60 fps game is shown as it is. |
| **Method** | **Neural**, the trained network. **Blend** and **Repeat** are the baselines to compare it with. |
| **Content Frame Cadence** | **Auto** measures how often a game makes a new frame. Force 1 to 4 when a game is misjudged (30 fps games: 2). |
| **Working Size** | The most pixels the generator works at: up to 4K, 1440p, 1080p or 720p. Smaller is faster. |
| **Status On Screen** | The method, the rate, the frame size and a count of made frames, over the game. |
| **Recommended Settings** | On, 120 fps, Neural, Auto, up to 4K, status off. |

**Find it in three places:** the leftmost tab in RetroArch's XMB, the first entry of RGUI's main menu, and EmulationStation's menu. A choice made in one is the choice in all three.

The frame rate counter now tells the two apart: **Base Rate** is the game's own new frames a second, **FrameGen** the frames you see.

<a id="achievements"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/checkmark-circle.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> RetroAchievements

Sign in with your [retroachievements.org](https://retroachievements.org) account and earn achievements in the games that have them.

- **Sign in from the WebUI** (**Settings → Achievements**) or from RetroArch's own menu.
- **Your password is used once.** After the first sign-in RetroArch keeps a sign-in token, and the password is removed from your console's settings files. The WebUI never shows a saved password or token.
- **Switching accounts works first time.** A new user name or password replaces the previous account's token.
- **Hardcore mode is off by default.** Turn it on for the stricter rules: no save states, rewind, slow motion or cheats while playing.
- **Secure connections.** HTTPS now checks the server's certificate, so nothing on your network can stand in for RetroAchievements and read your sign-in.

<a id="netplay"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/game-chat.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> NetPlay

RetroArch is now built with networking, so **NetPlay** is here: host a game or join one over the network, from RetroArch's NetPlay menu. Its settings, including the NetPlay password, are also in the WebUI under **Settings → Netplay**, where passwords can be set but never read back.

The **Online Updater** is gone from RetroArch's menu: cores and assets come with each release, built for the PS5.

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from v1.0.0-beta.1

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content and any custom media.
2. **Save your PS2 games in-game**, to a memory card. Save states made with LRPS2 do not load in PS5SX2.
3. Merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the beta package into your installation, keeping your own files and configuration.
4. **Delete LRPS2's files**, which a merge leaves behind: `cores/pcsx2_libretro.so`, `cores/pcsx2_libretro.info` and `info/pcsx2_libretro.info`.
5. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch. If a PS2 playlist still names LRPS2, choose **PS5SX2** as its core once.
6. Sign in to RetroAchievements from **Settings → Achievements** in the WebUI, and choose your language in its header.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
