## PS5 RetroArch is a passion project, not a piracy project.

It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/home.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Game Forwarder. Your games, on the home screen.

Make a home screen tile for any game in your library, right from the WebUI. Each tile is a small title of its own: open it and RetroArch starts straight into that game, with the core you chose.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/assets/releases/v3.0.0-beta-3/game-forwarder.webp" width="960" alt="The WebUI's Game Forwarder page making a new tile: the picture changes from box art to 3D box, logo and cartridge, fills the square, and takes the system's icon in its corner">

1. Open **Game Forwarder** in the WebUI and choose **New tile**.
2. Choose the **system**, each listed with its console, then the **game**.
3. Set the **name** the home screen shows and the **core**; the game's own core is chosen for you.
4. Choose the **picture**: box art, 3D box, logo, cartridge or disc, screenshot, title screen or fan art. Fill the square or fit the picture inside it, and add the system's icon in the bottom right corner if you like.
5. Choose **Create tile**, then close RetroArch. Your tile appears on the home screen.

- **Close RetroArch when the game ends**, or carry on in your frontend afterwards: each tile keeps your choice.
- **Edit or delete a tile at any time.** A new name or picture, and a deleted tile, reach the home screen a few seconds after RetroArch closes.
- **Games in your content folder or on a USB or extended storage drive** can have a tile.
- **Tiles need [ShadowMountPlus](https://github.com/drakmor/ShadowMountPlus) 1.7 or later**, with its API on (its default). The page says so when ShadowMountPlus does not answer.

Forwarders made with other tools work too: they start PS5 RetroArch with `--rom <file>`, `--core <core>` and `--exit-after-game`, as the [README](https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch#readme) describes.

<a id="swanstation"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/systems/psx.webp" width="26" height="26" alt=""> SwanStation, a second PlayStation core

SwanStation joins Beetle PSX HW, which stays the PlayStation default. It draws with Vulkan on the same GPU device as RetroArch, and its recompiler runs natively on the PS5. Choose it for a game or a whole playlist from RetroArch's menu; its options are in the WebUI's **Settings**, under its core profile.

It reads the same BIOS files as Beetle PSX HW, from `system/`. **Use only a BIOS dumped from a PlayStation you own.**

<a id="melonds"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/systems/nds.webp" width="26" height="26" alt=""> melonDS DS, with your DualSense as its microphone

melonDS DS joins DeSmuME, which stays the Nintendo DS default.

- **The touchpad is the stylus**, as it is in DeSmuME.
- **Your DualSense is the microphone.** RetroArch now reads the controller's microphone for any core that asks for one. melonDS DS listens only while its microphone button is held, and a muted DualSense sends silence.
- **Turn the microphone off for every core** in RetroArch's **Settings → Audio → Microphone**.

<a id="frame-generation"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/video.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Frame Generation, paced to your display

- **Full speed on 60 Hz outputs.** In v2.0.0-beta-2, a TV or monitor without 120 Hz showed each picture twice, so games ran at half speed with broken sound. RetroArch now times the display's real refresh rate when it starts: on a 60 Hz output a 120 fps target becomes 60, and a 30 fps game still gets a frame made between its own. 120 Hz and VRR displays keep 120.
- **Off means off.** With Frame Generation turned off, each game frame is shown exactly once, whatever it was set to before.
- **On and off take effect at once.** Switching Frame Generation in the menus restarts the video output for a moment, as changing the Target Framerate already did.

<a id="achievements"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/checkmark-circle.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> RetroAchievements for FLAC-compressed discs

`chdman` compresses CD audio with FLAC by default. RetroArch can now open those CHD images itself to compute a disc's RetroAchievements hash. Before, the core played them, but the hash failed and achievements stayed off for those games.

<a id="library"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/library.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Your library, in EmulationStation's layout

- **Content folders take ES-DE's system names**, such as `content/psx`, `content/ps2` and `content/snes`, so `content/` is laid out as EmulationStation expects.
- **Folders from earlier builds are renamed for you** on the first start, or merged when nothing clashes. Your playlists, per-folder overrides, core options, shader presets and remaps follow them. Folders you named yourself stay as they are.
- **Archived cartridge games are found** for the Mega Drive, Master System, SG-1000, Game Gear, 32X, PC Engine and SuperGrafx, even though their cores also run disc systems.
- **EmulationStation shows your games from its very first start.** On a fresh install it used to stay empty until the second.
- **Add Game** puts each game in its system's ES-DE folder.

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/checkmark-circle.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Played on a Base PS5

Everything below ran on a Base PS5 from this series' builds.

| What | How it went |
| --- | --- |

| **SwanStation** | Booted the PlayStation BIOS and a disc with Vulkan, up to 59.9 new frames a second. |

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from v2.0.0-beta-2

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content and any custom media.
2. Merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the beta package into your installation, keeping your own files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch. **The first start renames your content folders** to ES-DE's names; your playlists and per-folder settings follow.
4. For Game Forwarder, **load ShadowMountPlus 1.7 or later** before opening the WebUI's **Game Forwarder** page.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
