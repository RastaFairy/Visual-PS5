<a id="stylus"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/edit.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> A stylus in your DualSense

Use the DualSense touchpad as a stylus in **DeSmuME** and **Azahar**.

| Gesture | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Slide a finger** | Move the stylus without touching the emulated screen |
| **Physically press the touchpad** | Touch the screen at the nib |
| **Hold the press and slide** | Drag across the touchscreen |
| **Release the press** | Lift the stylus |

**Press the touchpad to click.** Tapping its surface does not click.

<a id="qr-access"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/laptop.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Scan the code. Open your WebUI.

QR codes are generated from the console’s current IP address.

| Frontend | Where to find it |
| --- | --- |
| **Picker** | Press **Triangle**; press Triangle again or Circle to close |
| **RetroArch XMB** | **WebUI** tab immediately left of Settings |
| **RetroArch RGUI** | **WebUI** at the top of the main menu |
| **ES-DE** | **WebUI** in the main menu |

You can also open `http://<PS5-IP>:6769` from a phone or computer on the same local network.

<a id="upgrading"></a>

## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mihawk-99/PS5_RetroArch/main/webui/assets/fluent/arrow-sync.svg" width="26" height="26" alt=""> Upgrading from 0.6.7

1. **Back up your existing title folder**, especially configuration, saves, states, system files, content, and any custom media.
2. Merge the complete `PPSA99169` folder from the beta package into your installation. Preserve your existing user files and configuration.
3. Fully close and reopen PS5 RetroArch after updating.
4. Open **Games** to explore your collection, or **Download Media** to fill in its artwork and details.

**No games, BIOS files, console firmware, or decryption keys are supplied. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.**
