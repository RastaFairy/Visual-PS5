It exists so you can play the games you own on the console you own.

- **Piracy is not condoned.**
- **No games, BIOS files, console firmware or decryption keys are included, and they never will be.**
- **Use only legally obtained backups of games you own**, made yourself from your own discs, cartridges or digital purchases.
- **Use only BIOS and firmware files dumped from hardware you own.**
- **Requests for, or links to, games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are not welcome** in this project's issues or discussions.

## Two frontends, one RetroArch

- **A pre-screen when the title starts.** Move with the <kbd>D-pad</kbd>, start a frontend with <kbd>Cross</kbd>, close the title with <kbd>Circle</kbd>. Each card shows the frontend it starts, captured on the console.
- **Remember your choice.** Press <kbd>Square</kbd> to switch Remember on, and the title starts that frontend straight away from then on. Hold <kbd>L1</kbd> while the title starts to come back to the pre-screen. The WebUI’s new **Start with** setting changes it from your phone or computer: *Ask every time*, *RetroArch* or *EmulationStation*.
- **Quitting goes where you expect.** Quitting RetroArch or EmulationStation returns to the pre-screen. With a frontend remembered, it closes the title, as RetroArch alone always did.
- **Each frontend runs on its own.** Switching restarts the title as the frontend you chose, in about a second, so each one has the console’s full memory and nothing of the other stays behind.

## EmulationStation on PS5

ES-DE 3.5.0, built for the console.

- **Alekfull NX by default**, with ES-DE’s own Linear theme also included.
- **Smooth browsing at 58–60 fps**, after an OpenGL driver fix that removed a full-screen cache flush from every frame.
- **Sound.** ES-DE’s navigation sounds play through the PS5’s audio output.
- **Your library, not a second one.** Its systems and game lists come from RetroArch’s playlists every time it starts. A game you add or remove in RetroArch appears or goes in EmulationStation; nothing is scanned twice.
- **Every game starts in RetroArch**, with RetroArch’s cores, saves, states, shaders, overlays, remaps and settings. Close the content and you are back in EmulationStation, on the same game, with its play count and time recorded.
- **Favorites and Last Played.** RetroArch’s favourites, history and play time fill EmulationStation’s Favorites and Last Played collections, and a favourite set in either frontend shows here.

## Up to four controllers

- **One controller per signed-in PS5 user.** Player 1 is whoever started the title; each other signed-in user’s controller becomes the next player.
- **Players follow the signed-in users.** A user who signs in during a session takes the first free player within about a second; one who signs out is let go.
- **Per-controller rumble and DualSense haptics.**

To add a player, turn on another controller and sign in as another user or a guest.

## The WebUI

- **Verified updates from the console.** Download a published release and check it; choosing Install closes RetroArch, installs it and closes the title. Open it again to run the new version. Your configuration, saves, states and content are kept.
- **BIOS alerts** tell you when a core’s BIOS file is missing.
- **Start with** chooses the frontend the title opens on.

## Display

The Vulkan driver now offers **3840×2160, 2560×1440 and 1920×1080**, not 4K alone. RetroArch still starts at the largest; set `video_fullscreen_x` and `video_fullscreen_y` to choose a smaller one. Each size was checked filling the TV.

## Fixes

- **Manual Scan no longer crashes** when you open Content Directory again after creating a playlist. The same fault affected the menu’s search, Explore filtering and Load Content’s return to the last folder; all of them are fixed, and the build now refuses the kind of missing function that caused it.
- **Logs you can send with a bug report.** Each launch keeps its own `trace.txt` (the previous one is `trace.1.txt`), and a game started from EmulationStation logs to `retroarch-game.log`, so it no longer overwrites RetroArch’s own `retroarch.log`.

## Tested scope and alpha limits

**Verified on a base PS5, firmware 13.40**

- The pre-screen, both frontends and every handover between them: choosing, quitting, Remember, **Start with**, and a launch straight into a remembered frontend.
- A game started from EmulationStation in RetroArch, closed through the Quick Menu’s **Close Content**, returning to EmulationStation with its play recorded.
- Two controllers as players 1 and 2 in a racing game.
- RetroArch’s favourites and play time in EmulationStation’s collections.
- EmulationStation’s sound output, checked through its audio device status.
- The display sizes above, each filling the TV.

**Not yet verified**

- EmulationStation with more than one controller, and a controller joining or leaving during a RetroArch session.
- Holding <kbd>L1</kbd> on a real pad at launch; the pad read itself works.
- An update installed from the WebUI that closes the title instead of starting the pre-screen (covered by tests).
- EmulationStation’s scraper and other online features, and sound in video previews.
- The PS5 Pro, for the frontends.

This is an alpha release: compatibility varies by core, game and workload.

## Install or upgrade

1. **Back up** your existing title folder.
2. **Merge** the complete `PPSA99169` folder into your launcher’s installation location. It adds two folders, `es-de/` and `picker/`. Preserve your `config`, saves, states, system files and content; do not replace your configuration with a fresh default.
3. **Open the title.** It now opens on the pre-screen. To start RetroArch directly as before, choose it with Remember on, or set **Start with** to RetroArch in the WebUI.

No games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are included. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and system files dumped from hardware you own.
